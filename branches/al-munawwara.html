Newest Branch

Al-Munawwara Branch

The Al-Munawwara Branch represents the newest chapter in the SIS story. Opened to serve families in the Al-Munawwara District of Riyadh, this campus brings the full SIS educational vision to yet another community — with the same uncompromising standards of academic excellence.

As SIS continues to grow, the Al-Munawwara Branch demonstrates our commitment to making world-class international education accessible across Riyadh.
