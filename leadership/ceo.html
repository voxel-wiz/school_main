Dr. Syed N Masood

CEO & Managing Director

"Nurturing future-ready students through innovation, strong values, academic excellence, and a caring environment that inspires lifelong learning."

CEO's Message

Dr. Syed N Masood brings over two decades of educational leadership and management experience to Success International School. As the CEO and Managing Director, he is responsible for the overall strategic direction and operational excellence of all SIS branches.

Under his leadership, SIS has established a reputation for academic innovation and a student-centered approach. Dr. Masood emphasizes the importance of 21st-century skills, integrating technology, robotics, and coding into the core curriculum to prepare students for the complexities of the modern world.

Our Growing Community

"Today, we are proud to serve over 1,750+ students across three thriving branches. Our commitment remains firm: to provide affordable, world-class education that empowers every child to reach their full potential."

Dr. Masood is a firm believer in the power of a nurturing environment. He works closely with our team of 160+ dedicated educators to ensure that Success International School remains a second home where students feel safe, valued, and inspired to learn.

He is also leading the expansion of SIS, with new campuses and advanced facilities designed to meet the growing needs of our community in Riyadh and beyond.
