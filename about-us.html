About Us

Our Legacy

The Journey of Excellence

Our schools started with the opening of kindergarten and primary sections in the academic year 2014. Since then, we have grown into a multi-phase educational institution committed to nurturing the leaders of tomorrow.

2014

Founding Year

The journey began with the opening of kindergarten (Pre-K and KG1-3) and primary sections (Grades 1-6) for both boys and girls.

2016

Middle School Expansion

Consistent efforts led to the opening of the middle school section (Grades 7-9) for boys and girls, spanning until 2023.

2024

High School & New Building

A milestone year seeing the opening of high school sections (Grades 10-12) for both sections and the inauguration of our new state-of-the-art building.
