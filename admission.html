The Admission Journey

We follow a transparent and nurturing assessment process to ensure every student finds their right place in our community.

01

Submit Application Form

Complete and submit the application form online or in person.

02

Review of Documents

Our admissions team will review the submitted documents and academic records.

03

Entrance Assessments

Depending on the grade level, students may be required to undergo entrance assessments.

04

Interview

An interview with the student and parent(s) may be conducted, particularly for higher grade levels, to understand their goals and expectations.

05

Acceptance

Once all requirements are met, the student will be notified of their acceptance and admission into the school.

Eligibility Criteria by Grade

Kindergarten (Pre-K & KG1-3)

Children must meet the minimum age requirements set by the school for each grade. Age-appropriate assessments may be conducted to ensure that students are ready for the learning environment.

Primary School (Grades 1-6)

Admission is based on the student’s age and prior academic performance. Students may also undergo assessments in English, Mathematics, and general knowledge.

Middle School (Grades 7-9)

Students must meet the academic standards for admission, with an emphasis on basic literacy and numeracy skills. Students may be asked to submit previous academic records and complete assessments.

High School (Grades 10-12)

Admission to high school requires a successful academic record, typically with a minimum grade in core subjects. Students may need to complete assessments based on their selected curriculum track (IGCSE, SAT, etc.).

Entrance Tests & Interviews

To maintain our high academic standards, we conduct comprehensive assessments and personal interviews.

edit_note

Entrance Tests

Students may be assessed in English, Mathematics, and other subjects relevant to their grade level. These tests are designed to gauge the student’s academic ability and ensure they are placed in an appropriate grade.

record_voice_over

Interviews

An interview may be conducted with the student (and their parents) to understand their aspirations, social skills, and alignment with the school’s ethos. The interview helps ensure that the student’s educational and personal goals align with what our school offers.

Tuition Fees & Payment Plans

Our tuition fees are structured in line with international school standards, ensuring best-in-class educational resources and facilities.

Tuition Fees

Fees are determined by grade level and curriculum chosen. Detailed information about the exact tuition fees can be provided upon request. The fees include all regular academic costs, access to digital resources, and participation in most school activities.

Payment Plans

We offer flexible payment plans to accommodate the needs of our families. Tuition can be paid annually, semi-annually, or in quarterly installments. Payment methods include bank transfer, cheque, or online payments.

Additional Fees

Certain programs or activities, such as field trips, extracurricular clubs, and advanced courses, may incur additional fees. These fees will be communicated well in advance.
